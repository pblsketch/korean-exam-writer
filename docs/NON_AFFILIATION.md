# Non-affiliation

This project is an independent, open educational tool. It is **not affiliated
with or endorsed by**:

- the **Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation (KICE)** or the CSAT (수능).
  Official CSAT materials are referenced only as **metadata-only** public-board
  provenance for a non-reconstructive corpus study (see [PROVENANCE](PROVENANCE.md)).
  No official passage/item full text, item numbers, or answer keys are included.

Generated exam packets are **synthetic teaching drafts** requiring teacher review
before classroom use. They are not official exams and carry no claim of
equivalence to any official test's difficulty, fairness, or psychometric quality
(see [METHODOLOGY](METHODOLOGY.md) → Limitations).
